When the inspection happens

The move-out inspection happens within two business days of the resident handing in keys, and always before any vendor enters the unit. The property manager walks the unit with the maintenance lead. The resident is offered the chance to attend; attendance is offered, not required.

What gets recorded

  • Photographs of every room, not only the rooms with findings.
  • A reading for every meter, with a photograph of the dial.
  • Each finding against the move-in condition report: the room, the item, and whether it is wear or damage.
  • Keys, fobs and remotes returned, counted against the handover record.

Deadlines

The itemized move-out statement goes to the resident within 21 days of move-out, counted from the day the keys are returned. A turn that cannot start within two days of the inspection is marked Stuck on the Turn Prep canvas so the crew sees it the same morning.
